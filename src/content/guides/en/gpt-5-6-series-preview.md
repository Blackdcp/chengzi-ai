## Deep Tech Dive: The Architecture of Sol, Terra, and Luna

The architecture behind the GPT-5.6 series represents a significant leap from the GPT-4 era. OpenAI has moved towards a more refined Mixture of Experts (MoE) system, which significantly reduces the inference latency while drastically improving the depth of response.

### 1. The Sol Model: The Apex of Reasoning
Sol is designed for maximum compute and reasoning depth. It doesn't just predict the next token; it simulates multiple logical pathways before generating an output. This "System 2" thinking approach allows Sol to write complex backend architectures, debug intricate smart contracts, and analyze vast datasets without losing context. 

### 2. The Terra Model: The Daily Workhorse
Terra is designed for enterprise integration and everyday use. It balances speed and capability. For content creation, data summarization, and mid-level coding, Terra is faster than previous generation models while maintaining the reasoning quality of a GPT-4 class model.

### 3. The Luna Model: Edge and Efficiency
Luna is the lightweight model of the family. It is optimized for sub-second response times, making it perfect for real-time customer service bots, basic data parsing, and mobile-first integrations.
