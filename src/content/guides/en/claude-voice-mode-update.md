## Deep Tech Dive: Why is Tool Use a Watershed Moment?

Making an AI speak isn't hard (there are plenty of great TTS models), but enabling an AI to **use tools during a live voice conversation** is incredibly challenging.

### 1. Ultra-Low Latency Function Routing
During a real-time voice chat, the AI has hundreds of milliseconds to decide: Does this sentence just need a verbal reply, or should it trigger an external API? Claude, powered by its upgraded Sonnet model, achieves this intent recognition with extremely low latency.

### 2. Context Memory and Cross-App Synergy
Claude can remember temporary instructions given via voice and build a contextual bridge between a Slack search result and a Gmail sending action, ensuring it doesn't "hallucinate" or drop tasks mid-chain.
