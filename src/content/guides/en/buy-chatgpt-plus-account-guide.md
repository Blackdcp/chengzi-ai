Buying ChatGPT Plus access is not only about the price. The safer decision is to match the product type to your real use case: temporary access, your own account renewal, or a ready-to-use account.

## Quick decision

- Choose a monthly pass if you want a low-cost short-term Plus experience.
- Choose renewal if you already have your own ChatGPT account and want to keep Plus on that account.
- Choose a ready-to-use account if you do not want to register or configure an account yourself.
- Choose Pro only if you have heavy AI coding, file analysis, or deep research needs.

## What to check before ordering

1. Confirm which email will receive delivery.
2. Read the product detail page and FAQ.
3. Make sure the product type matches your account situation.
4. Do not order renewal for an account that is restricted or unavailable.
5. Keep your order ID for support.
