## A New King in Video Generation: FLUX 3

The video generation space just got a powerful new player! Today, Black Forest Labs launched the **FLUX 3 multimodal foundation model** via Early Access.

While Sora delays its public release and other models struggle with coherence and length, FLUX 3 dropped a bombshell. Unlike models that only generate video frames, FLUX 3 uses a unified architecture to learn images, video, and audio simultaneously. Most impressively, it can generate up to 20 seconds of video in a single pass **with natively matched audio**.

This means short video creators no longer need to scavenge sound libraries for sound effects. When a glass shatters in the generated video, the crisp sound of breaking glass is generated simultaneously.

## Deep Tech Dive: What Makes FLUX 3 Different?

Video models have exploded over the past year, but FLUX 3 makes two core architectural breakthroughs:

### 1. Native Multimodal Joint Training
Historically, video audio was a two-step process (run the diffusion model, then run a separate audio model), leading to sync issues. FLUX 3 maps both video frames and waveforms into the same latent space for joint training, achieving true audiovisual synchronization.

### 2. Understanding the Physical World (FLUX-mimic)
FLUX 3's ambition goes beyond TikTok videos; it aims for Embodied AI. Partnering with robotics company mimic, over 95% of its training compute went into video prediction. This ensures its generated videos adhere strictly to real-world physics (gravity, collisions, occlusions), allowing the model to eventually drive industrial robotic arms.
