Ready account and renewal solve different problems. Choosing the wrong one usually causes delivery delays or support questions.

## Ready account

A ready account is for users who do not want to register or configure an account. After delivery, you follow the login instructions and start using it.

Best for:

- New users without a ChatGPT account.
- Users who need a backup account.
- Temporary Plus usage.
- Users who want fewer setup steps.

## Renewal

Renewal is for users who already have their own ChatGPT account and want to continue using Plus on that account.

Best for:

- Users with an existing account.
- Users who want to keep history and settings.
- Long-term users who prefer their own account.

## Which one is safer?

Neither is universally better. The safe choice is the one that matches your account status.

- No account: choose ready account.
- Own account: choose renewal.
- Heavy usage: compare Plus with Pro first.
