## Why Shared Gemini Panels and Proxy API Keys Fail in Antigravity

1. **OAuth Subscription Binding**: Antigravity authenticates your Google account directly to verify active Google AI Pro or Ultra entitlements.
2. **Strict Multi-IP Risk Controls**: Logging shared accounts from multiple regions triggers immediate Google verification locks or account disabling.
3. **Agent Context Economics**: Multi-step agent workflows routinely consume hundreds of thousands of input tokens per session. Paying per token via raw API scales costs rapidly, whereas flat-rate **Ultra 5X** and **Ultra 20X** subscriptions provide far better predictable ROI for heavy coders.
