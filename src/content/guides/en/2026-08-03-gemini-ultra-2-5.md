The leap from the previous 1M-2M token limits to an astonishing 10M tokens is not merely a scaling of existing transformer architecture—it's a fundamental redesign of memory allocation.

Gemini Ultra 2.5 leverages an advanced **Ring Attention** mechanism coupled with highly optimized Sparse Memory Retrieval. Traditional transformers scale quadratically with context length, leading to catastrophic memory exhaustion. It eliminates the loss of nuance typically seen when video is compressed into textual descriptions.
